WHITEBOARD - BAN DONG GOI CHAY TREN LAPTOP (Windows 10/11 x64)
===============================================================

Khong can cai gi them: da kem Node.js, PostgreSQL va thu vien VC++.

LAN DAU TREN MAY MOI
  1. Chuot phai setup-firewall.bat > Run as administrator  (mo port cho iPad)
  2. Nhay dup start.bat  (lan dau mat ~10 giay de tao database)

MOI LAN CHAY
  - start.bat : bat PostgreSQL + server + client, in ra dia chi cho iPad
  - stop.bat  : tat het

TREN IPAD
  - Tat Wi-Fi, cam day LAN (qua switch) vao laptop
  - Mo Safari:  http://<IP-laptop>:5173   (start.bat in san dia chi)

APP WATERFALL DESIGNER (cung laptop)
  - Muc Drawtogether Bridge > bam "Noi bo (LAN)"  (http://localhost:3001)

DU LIEU
  - data\pg    : database (phong, net ve, su kien Performance, chu ky).
  - data\media : anh/video nen cua Performance.
  - Xoa thu muc data = xoa sach du lieu. Cap nhat goi: chep de len, GIU data\.
  - logs\   : log PostgreSQL / tao DB khi co loi.

DOI PORT
  - Sua config.bat. WB_SERVER_PORT phai giu 3001 (client da build san port nay).

LOI THUONG GAP
  - iPad khong vao duoc: chua chay setup-firewall.bat, hoac sai IP.
  - "LOI PostgreSQL": port 5433 bi chiem -> doi PG_PORT trong config.bat.
  - Port 3001/5173 bi chiem: tat app dang dung port do (vd ban dev).
